Design Logic

The T is one rigid piece — it slides in and out but never changes shape. The crank-slider controls radial position only. At θ=0° the T-head protrudes past the disc edge by the fixed pin protrusion amount. At θ=90° it's fully retracted. The 5 guide blocks (gold) each sit between two adjacent T-heads, guiding both simultaneously.
